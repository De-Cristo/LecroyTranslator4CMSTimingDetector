\newcommand{\DPTitle}{MCP-referenced timing calibration of CMS BTL prototype bars}
\newcommand{\DPShortTitle}{MCP-referenced timing calibration}
\newcommand{\DPContact}{cms-dpg-conveners-mtd@cern.ch}
\newcommand{\DPAbstract}{%
A timing-calibration procedure is studied using CMS barrel timing layer (BTL)
prototype scintillator bars in the September 2025 beam test. A microchannel-plate
photomultiplier (MCP-PMT), digitized by an oscilloscope, provides an external
arrival-time reference. A trigger link identifies coincident events in the
BTL readout and the oscilloscope. Segment-dependent clock alignment and a
locally weighted smoothing correction are used to map the reference into the
BTL time coordinate.

The dependence of the measured arrival time on pulse charge is described by
quadratic functions of inverse charge for four silicon-photomultiplier channels
on two reference bars. These functions provide an empirical time-walk
calibration over the measured charge range. Clock-corrected profiles provide
the reference for the companion dual-threshold study; archived fits are
retained to document the change in calibration procedure. Timing-difference distributions
illustrate the validation strategy and the need to distinguish intrinsic
reference-detector resolution from residual synchronization effects.
This draft documents the available results and identifies the remaining
selection, uncertainty and reproducibility checks before publication.}
