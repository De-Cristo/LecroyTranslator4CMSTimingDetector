\begin{frame}{Abstract}
\normalsize
Several analyses of the 2025 CMS barrel timing layer (BTL) test-beam data
benefit from a reliable particle-arrival time reference. A Hamamatsu
microchannel-plate photomultiplier (MCP-PMT), digitized by a digital oscilloscope,
provides an external reference for a subset of the data. Its limited acceptance
and acquisition rate motivate transferring the timing calibration to a BTL
reference module.

A trigger link through the FEBd readout correlates events in the two streams.
Clock alignment and locally weighted smoothing correct their low-frequency
relative drift. Quadratic functions of inverse charge then describe the
time walk of the left and right channels of a bar.

The calibrated reference bar provides a time reference for BTL analyses.
The time difference between a bar in the module under test and the reference
bar, before and after walk correction, tests the calibration. This FEBd-based
synchronization procedure is specific to the test-beam setup.
\end{frame}

\PlotSlide{After synchronization, the MCP defines the calibration time}{mcp_waveform}{
Fit the MCP waveform to estimate its pulse time.

Within the scope waveform, define
\[
\delta_{\rm MCP}=t_{\rm peak}-t_{\rm trigger}.
\]
For SiPM channel $i$, use
\[
\Delta t_i=t_i-g(u)-\delta_{\rm MCP}.
\]
The charge-dependent part of this difference is the walk.
}{The waveform and fitted template determine the MCP pulse time. $u$ is the scope-trigger timestamp.
A fixed cable/geometry offset is irrelevant to the shape; clock-rate conversion over the short waveform interval is neglected.}

\begin{frame}{Measure the reference-module channels' time walk versus charge}
\fontsize{9.5}{11}\selectfont
\textbf{Separate threshold settings before selection and fitting.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Require exactly five TOFHIR hits and a unique MCP match:
the left and right channels of one bar in the reference module and one bar
in the module under test, plus the dedicated trigger channel.
\item Within each setting, require clock quality and finite positive charge,
$\Delta I$ and threshold-rise time. Keep charge above the spectrum's left half-maximum boundary.
\item Form 20 uniform $1/Q$ bins. Clip timing outliers at
$5(1.4826\,\mathrm{MAD})$ from each bin's median; require at least five entries.
\item Fit the bin means with weights $1/\mathrm{SEM}^2$:
\[
W_i(Q)=a_i+b_i/Q+c_i/Q^2.
\]
\end{enumerate}
$Q$ is the measured ADC charge proxy, not energy in MeV.
SEM denotes the standard error of the mean.
\Source{Reference module: bar 8; module under test: bar 3. Combine files only at identical scan settings.
\Code{Q_Walk/qwalk_t0_data.py}{GitHub: selected reference sample}.}
\end{frame}
